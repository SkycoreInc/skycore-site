01 — Cloud Migration

Seamlessly move to the cloud — zero downtime

Cloud adoption is one of the most impactful investments a business can make — and one of the easiest to get wrong. Our migration process is methodical: assess, plan, execute, optimize.

We work across Azure and hybrid architectures — whichever combination fits your workloads, compliance requirements, and budget.

  • Infrastructure assessment & planning
    Full inventory of your current stack before a single byte moves.
  • Data migration & application modernization
    Lift-and-shift or full re-architecture — whatever makes business sense.
  • Multi-cloud strategy & optimization
    Avoid lock-in, control costs, match workloads to the right provider.
